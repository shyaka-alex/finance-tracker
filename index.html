About Spendwise

Spendwise was built by Shyaka Alex as a project for the African Leadership University course Building Responsive UI. It is a simple, accessible way for students in Rwanda to manage everyday spending, from lunch and moto rides to textbooks and fees.

It is made with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript only. Your data never leaves your browser.
