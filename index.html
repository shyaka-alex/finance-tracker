        About

        Why Spendwise exists and who built it.

        Purpose

        Students spend in many small amounts, like lunch, moto rides and printing, and it is easy to lose track. Spendwise records every transaction, groups it by category, shows the last seven days at a glance and warns you before you pass the monthly cap you set.

        It is built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript only. Your data never leaves your browser.

        • Regex-powered live search with highlighted matches
        • Inline editing, sorting and safe delete confirmation
        • Budget cap with spoken updates for screen reader users
        • Three currencies with manual rates
        • JSON import and export with validation
